About

I am an independent researcher interested in how to make language models smaller, cheaper to train, and more reliable, with a focus on low-resource languages. My perspective is that if we can train high-performing language models for low-resource languages, similar techniques might also improve language models for high-resource languages. I am also interested in AI safety and alignment, and in methods for building secure guardrails and safeguards against the dangers of current AI technology.

Key research areas:

  • Efficient ML and NLP
  • Optimization
  • AI Safety and Alignment
  • Low-resource NLP
  • LLM Evaluation and Benchmarking

Available for research collaborations, internships, jobs, and graduate study (MSc and PhD).
